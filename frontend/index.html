GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about studying in Türkiye.

What are the general admission requirements for Turkish Universities?
International applicants need a High School Diploma or Bachelor's Degree transcript, a valid Passport copy, and passport photos. For English-medium programs, TOEFL or university English placement test is accepted.
Are partial or full scholarships available for international students?
Yes! Through Admission Turkey, international students automatically receive guaranteed 25% to 60% partial tuition scholarships at partner universities upon application submission.
Do I need to take YÖS or SAT exams to study in Turkey?
No, for private foundation universities in Turkey, YÖS or SAT exams are NOT mandatory. You can apply directly using your high school grades.
How long does the student visa process take?
Once you receive your Official Acceptance Letter, the student visa application at the Turkish Embassy/Consulate usually takes between 2 to 4 weeks.
